## Overview

A responsive, SEO-optimized website that presents the school to parents and prospective students: who the school is, what it offers, and how to get in touch. Designed and developed end to end as a solo project, from design through deployment on a custom domain.

### Goals

- Give parents a clear, trustworthy first impression of the school
- Load fast and work well on low-end phones and slow mobile networks
- Rank for local searches such as "English medium school in Manvi"
- Be easy for school staff to keep up to date

# Milton Public School Website

A production website for **Milton Public School, Manvi**, created to strengthen the school’s digital presence and make essential information easier for students, parents, staff, and visitors to find.

[![Visit Website](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live%20Website-Visit-1f6feb?style=for-the-badge)](https://miltonpublicschoolmanvi.in/)

## Project overview

The website serves as a central online information hub for Milton Public School. It presents the school’s identity, academics, student life, and admissions information through a clear, visitor-focused experience.

Milton Public School is an English-medium school in Manvi, Karnataka, offering education from Baby Class through 10th Standard under the Karnataka State Board. The site describes the school’s background, curriculum, activities, and admissions process.

## Key features

- **School information:** Introduction to the school, its educational approach, and its community.

- **Academic overview:** Information about the Karnataka State Board curriculum and the classes served.

- **School-life content:** Highlights of classroom learning, sports, karate, NCC, cultural activities, and educational trips.

- **Admissions information:** An overview of the admission journey, from initial inquiry and campus visit to document submission and enrollment.

- **Announcements and updates:** A digital channel for timely school notices, events, and announcements.

- **Responsive experience:** Designed to support visitors using desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
